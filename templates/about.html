THE IDEA

A clearer way to stay consistent.

Every training session tells a story: the exercises you chose, the weight you lifted, the reps you completed and the effort you put in. FitHub gives those details a home.

It is designed for everyday gym-goers who want a straightforward place to record their work, understand their habits and see progress over time. No complicated setup is needed to get started.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

01 / TRAIN

Record every set.

Save exercises, reps, weight, warm-up sets and completion so your workout history stays useful.

02 / FUEL

Keep an eye on nutrition.

Log meals and follow calories and macros alongside your training routine.

03 / GROW

Make progress visible.

Use your workout history, weight entries, charts and goals to understand how things change.

OUR APPROACH

Useful first. Yours always.

Fitness is personal, and your records should be too. FitHub is built around practical tracking, clear information and giving you control of your own data.

FitHub is a training and wellness tracking tool, not a medical service. Use it to support your routine, and speak with a qualified professional for medical or nutrition advice.
